Hoops

How to play

  • Move: drag anywhere on the left half of the screen. Push to the edge to sprint.
  • Shoot: hold SHOOT to rise up, let go at the top of your jump. The green band on the meter is a perfect release. Near the rim while driving, SHOOT becomes a layup or dunk.
  • Move button: crossover. Pull the stick toward half court for a step-back, push at your defender for a spin.
  • Defense: SHOOT becomes BLOCK (jump and contest), MOVE becomes STEAL. Reaching and missing leaves you off balance.
  • Rules: 2s and 3s, first to the target wins. 12-second shot clock. After a defensive rebound or steal you must clear the ball past the arc.
  • Keyboard: WASD or arrows, Shift to sprint, J or Space = SHOOT/BLOCK, K = MOVE/STEAL, P = pause.
